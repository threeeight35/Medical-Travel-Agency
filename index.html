How It Works

A structured, 8-step patient coordination pathway with medical boundary clarity.

1

Submit an Initial Enquiry

Complete our confidential enquiry form or reach out directly to our Australian or UAE desk via phone or WhatsApp.

2

Tell Us Which Treatment You Wish to Explore

Specify your medical specialty or procedure of interest, your preferred travel timeframe, and any initial questions.

3

Explore Options & Obtain Independent Quotations

We present transparent package proposals from accredited hospitals with surgeon profiles, itemised estimates, and recovery schedules.

4

Review the Treating Facility and Doctor Credentials

Evaluate surgeon qualifications, licensing, case portfolios, and hospital accreditations before making any formal commitment.

5

Confirm Your Treatment Plan & Travel Arrangements

Schedule procedure dates, arrange flights, book Luxury 5-Star Hotels, and confirm your dedicated Luxury VIP Cars logistics.

6

Arrive in Türkiye with Coordinated Transfers

Private airport meet-and-greet, check-in at your hotel, and orientation by your dedicated English-speaking care coordinator.

7

Undergo Treatment and Recover Comfortably

In-person hospital accompanying liaison, procedure performance by your specialist, in-hospital care, and relaxing hotel recovery.

8

Return Home with Documentation & Continuity Support

Final doctor review, fit-to-fly clearance, comprehensive English medical records, and digital follow-up coordination with your local GP.
