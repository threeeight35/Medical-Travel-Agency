01 / Dental
Restorative & Aesthetic Dentistry

Dental Treatments & Smile Rehabilitation

Full-arch reconstructions, titanium implants, CAD/CAM zirconia crowns, and laminate veneers performed by specialist prosthodontists and oral maxillofacial surgeons.

Dental Implants All-on-4 All-on-6 Full-Mouth Rehabilitation Porcelain Veneers Laminate Veneers Zirconia Crowns E-Max Crowns Dental Crowns Smile Makeover Teeth Whitening Root Canal Treatment Dental Fillings Gum Treatment Tooth Extraction

Clinical Pathway & Diagnostics

Every dental plan begins with 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scans in Istanbul to assess bone volume, nerve positioning, and restorative feasibility.

  • Digital 3D CAD/CAM smile design
  • Swiss & German implant systems
  • Temporary prosthesis on Day 1

Suitability & Patient Questions

Ideal for patients with missing teeth, severe enamel erosion, or failing bridge work seeking permanent restoration.

Questions to ask:

"What is the brand and guarantee of the implant fixtures? Do I require sinus lifting or bone augmentation?"

Recommended Stay & Follow-up

Recommended stay: 5 to 7 days in Istanbul for initial placement. Osseointegration period: 3 months before permanent zirconia bridge seating.

02 / Aesthetic
Aesthetic & Reconstructive Surgery

Plastic & Cosmetic Surgery

Open preservation rhinoplasty, deep plane facelifts, blepharoplasty, ergonomic breast augmentation, and body contouring by European Board-certified plastic surgeons.

Preservation Rhinoplasty Deep Plane Facelift & Neck Lift Breast Augmentation (Motiva) High-Definition Liposuction

Surgical Standards

Conducted strictly in JCI-accredited operating suites under general anaesthesia with full post-operative inpatient monitoring.

Recovery & Safety

Cast and splint removal at Day 6-7. Fit-to-fly assessment conducted prior to departure with certified medical travel release.

Recommended Stay

Recommended stay: 7 to 9 nights in Istanbul for safe healing before international flight.

03 / Trichology
Follicular Unit Extraction & DHI

Hair Restoration & Follicular Transfer

Sapphire micro-FUE and Direct Hair Implantation (DHI) using Choi implanter pens for maximum graft survival, natural angulation, and dense hairline design.

Sapphire FUE (Up to 4,500 Grafts) DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) Beard & Eyebrow Restoration

Trichology Protocol

Doctor extraction and opening of channels with real sapphire blades, minimising trauma and accelerating scabbing recovery.

Clinical First Wash

Conducted in hospital on Day 2 by clinical nurses. Full aftercare package with specialized lotions and post-op care guidelines.

Recommended Stay

Recommended stay: 3 to 4 nights in Istanbul with full post-op release.
